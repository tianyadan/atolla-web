## The problem: more AI subscriptions, more friction

Many developers now pay for Claude, Codex and Cursor at once. Each is great on its own, but together:

- **Constant switching** — three terminals, three windows, and "which window was that task in?"
- **Split context** — tools don't know about each other, so you re-explain everything.
- **Hard to manage** — who is running, who is stuck, who is waiting for your approval?
- **Away from the desk, out of control** — a long task needs a permission and just sits there.

## How Atolla helps

### 1. One entry for three engines

Atolla supports Claude Code, Codex CLI and Cursor Agent. Each agent binds one engine, and every CLI's output becomes the same event stream — **no platform switching, no hunting for interruptions.** See [3.1](/docs/3-1-multi-engine).

### 2. Deeply customizable agents

Give agents roles, personas, workspaces and permission policies, then add skills from the skill store to build a cross-engine team. See [3.2](/docs/3-2-agents).

### 3. Safety-first execution

Every tool action passes the permission gateway: Hard Deny can't be bypassed, agent permissions apply independently, and anything that can't be decided safely comes to you. See [3.5](/docs/3-5-approval).

### 4. Control from anywhere

Deploy the relay on your own server and your phone can check status, continue chats and approve commands, with Live Activity and Bark notifications. See [3.4](/docs/3-4-relay).

### 5. Your data stays yours

Engines, workspaces and platform data live on your computer; the relay you host keeps only per-instance cache mirrors and a mailbox. See [4.1](/docs/4-1-data-security).
